# Job Collector context

A Job Position can have several Source postings. The existing matcher evaluates posting descriptions and summarizes results on the Job Position.

The accepted master CV design makes the selected Person’s CRM records authoritative for matching. The workspace owner is the suggested assignment. The first delivery keeps the current assessment method while moving the résumé into editable records.

In the accepted design each Person has a separate current assessment for each assessed Source posting. The Job Position summary uses the selected Person’s assessments.

Experience entries and projects supply evidence for CV skills. A project can exist independently of employment.

Skills are shared within the workspace. Each evidence item identifies the Person and master CV whose experience supports those skills.

The first delivery links all legacy Source postings and Job Positions to the workspace owner’s Person, including records without assessments.

The job matching design extracts requirements before evaluating a Person. Each satisfied requirement cites master CV records. The Person reviews AI recommendations before they change the master CV.

Candidate qualifications determine the weighted fitScore. Responsibilities and team context also inform AI recommendations.

Job collection uses one Person’s master CV Target role across all boards in a run. An explicit assignment takes precedence over the workspace owner default.
