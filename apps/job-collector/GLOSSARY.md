# Job Collector glossary

- **Master CV** — A Person’s editable source of career information.
- **Target role** — The role the Person wants to pursue.
- **Role held** — A historical role on an experience entry.
- **Assigned Person** — The CRM Person whose master CV the matching workflow uses.
- **Current assessment** — The latest saved match result for one Person and one Source posting.
- **Evidence item** — One achievement from an experience entry or project that CV skills can cite.
- **Skill** — A workspace record that CVs and future job requirements can reference.
- **Skill coverage** — The share of distinct requested skills or permitted skill alternatives matched for a priority tier.
- **fitScore** — The percentage of available requirement points earned by the assigned Person for a Source posting. Each satisfied must-have earns two points and each satisfied nice-to-have earns one.
- **Candidate qualification** — A criterion the employer asks the candidate to meet, established by the posting text and its section heading.
